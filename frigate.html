At the start of your turn ▼
  • Recharge Energy
  • Refresh any exhausted Technologies
  • Move any Modules (up to Speed distance)
  • Destroy any Modules that do not meet their requirements
  • Build any 1 Technology from your Hand (apply its On Build effect)
During your turn ▼
  • Activate Technologies, Deployable or Module effects (pay Energy where required)
    • Unless otherwise noted, after activating a Technology, Deployable or Module it is Exhausted.
  • Some Technologies create Deployables and Modules
    • Deployables are placed on the board represented by a token. They have a Hull value, Duration until they are destroyed and Speed that they may move each turn. Most Deployables also have conditions for removal and effects they can activate for free.
    • Modules are placed on the board represented by a token. Modules have a Hull value, they generally use your ships Shield and they have requirements they must meet. As long as these req. are met their effects and/or abilities can be used.
